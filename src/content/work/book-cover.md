**Client:** Library of Congress

**Disciplines:** Graphic Design, Editorial Design, Typography

### Project Overview

This project reimagines **Pride and Prejudice** through a series of modern, minimalist cover designs. The goal was to create distinct visual identities that feel contemporary yet timeless while preserving the novel's romantic wit and literary elegance.

### Design Process

Research began with exploring historical and contemporary book cover systems. Through sketching and digital development in Illustrator, I refined multiple concepts with close attention to hierarchy, symbolism, and compositional balance. Each cover emphasizes restraint, clarity, and typographic sophistication.
