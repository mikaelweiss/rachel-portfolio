**Client:** Editorial Interpretation Project

**Disciplines:** Editorial Design, Typography, Illustration

**Overview**

This editorial publication interprets *"Compensating Blessings"* by Gerald Causse. The design translates themes of faith, sacrifice, and promised blessings into a cohesive visual narrative.

**Process**

Original illustrations were created to symbolically reinforce the message of the text. Careful typographic hierarchy, pacing, and layout decisions ensure clarity while maintaining a reverent tone. The final booklet balances structured composition with expressive imagery to unify concept and craftsmanship.
